Presentation is one of the important skills which a researcher should have and a graduate student should keep learning and practicing. Presentations are often made for discussing research ideas or results on research meetings or conferences. A good presentation certainly helps much to delivering the ideas to the audience.

Scientific presentations have some differences with other kinds of presentations, sharing some common rules. I find a very good lecture from iBioSeminars by [Dr. Susan McConnell](https://med.stanford.edu/profiles/susan-mcconnell). Dr. McConnel gives helpful advice on preparing and presenting an effective scientific talk and presentation including choosing fonts, colors and slide styles, ways to structure the talk and more.

The lecture: Designing effective scientific presentations. It is also available on [Youtube](http://www.youtube.com/ibioseminars#p/search/3/Hp7Id3Yb9XQ).

The website also provides the [Slides](http://media.hhmi.org/ibio/mcconnell/mcconnell_powerpoint_pt1.pdf) and [MP4 format video](http://media.hhmi.org/ibio/mcconnell/mcconnell.mp4) and more to download.
